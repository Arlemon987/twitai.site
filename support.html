Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common Twit AI questions.

What is Twit AI?

Twit AI is an AI-powered tool that helps users generate suggested replies to posts on X/Twitter. It is available as a web application and browser extension.

Does Twit AI automatically post replies?

No. Twit AI generates suggested replies for you. The browser extension does not automatically publish replies to X/Twitter. You can review and copy the generated reply before posting it yourself.

Are my submitted tweets stored on your server?

No. Submitted tweet/post content is not stored in the Twit AI server or database. Relevant content may be stored locally in your browser for application functionality.

Where are my generated replies stored?

Generated replies may be stored locally in your browser. They are not intentionally maintained in a Twit AI server-side database.

Does Twit AI collect my browsing history?

Twit AI does not intentionally collect or store unrelated browsing history. The browser extension processes the information required for its intended functionality.

How does Google login work?

Twit AI may use Google/Firebase authentication. The authentication process can provide information such as your name, email address, profile information, and a unique account identifier. Twit AI does not receive your Google password.

Why can't I generate more replies?

Your account may have reached its available usage limit or your subscription may not currently provide access to additional generations. Check your account and subscription status, then contact support if the issue continues.

What should I do if payment is not verified?

Make sure the transaction information you submitted is correct and that the transaction has been completed on the relevant network. If your payment still has not been verified, contact support with your account email and transaction information.

Can I clear my locally stored tweet history?

Yes. You can clear Twit AI's site data or browser extension storage through your browser settings. Clearing local storage may permanently remove locally stored tweets, generated replies, and preferences.

Is Twit AI affiliated with X/Twitter?

Twit AI is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp. unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Extension Troubleshooting

If the Twit AI browser extension is not working correctly, try these steps first.

1. Refresh the X/Twitter page after installing or updating the extension.
2. Make sure you are logged into your Twit AI account.
3. Check that the extension is enabled in your browser.
4. Make sure your browser is updated to a supported version.
5. If the reply interface does not appear, refresh X/Twitter and try again.
6. If the problem continues, contact Twit AI support with details about the issue.

Still need help?

If you cannot find an answer here, contact the Twit AI support team. Please include your account email and a clear description of the problem so we can understand the issue.
